# MediSync — Research Index

This research collection supports the design of **MediSync**, a digital healthcare coordination solution focused on improving the accessibility and quality of public healthcare services in rural and underserved communities.

The selected resources were reviewed across key areas of the SIH26133 problem statement, including rural healthcare access, specialist availability, telemedicine, diagnostics, health records, healthcare-worker workflows, referrals, follow-up and healthcare equity.

# Research-to-Feature Overview

The literature was used as a supporting input for understanding the problem space and informing MediSync's system design.

## 1. Rural & Underserved Healthcare Access

**Research insight:** Rural populations can face geographic, accessibility and healthcare-service delivery constraints.

**MediSync response:** Remote consultation, Nearby Care and assisted healthcare workflows.

---

## 2. Primary Healthcare & Specialist Access

**Research insight:** Digital-health and telemedicine interventions can help connect patients and primary-care settings with healthcare professionals beyond their immediate location.

**MediSync response:** Doctor discovery, appointment scheduling and Video, Audio and Text consultations.

---

## 3. Diagnostics & Service Coordination

**Research insight:** Primary-care facilities may face limitations in diagnostic availability, specialist interpretation and diagnostic-service coordination.

**MediSync response:** Diagnostics module, test booking and coordination with nearby diagnostic facilities.

---

## 4. Health Records & Continuity of Care

**Research insight:** Timely access to portable and organized health information can support continuity of care, particularly when patients interact with multiple healthcare facilities.

**MediSync response:** Health Records, Clinical Records and Health QR.

---

## 5. Referrals & Follow-up

**Research insight:** Digital referral systems can support more structured coordination between referring and receiving healthcare facilities compared with manual processes.

**MediSync response:** Follow-ups and coordinated healthcare workflows.

---

## 6. Health Workers & Assisted Care

**Research insight:** Community and frontline health workers can play an important role in delivering and supporting digital healthcare services in underserved settings.

**MediSync response:** Dedicated Health Worker role and assisted patient–doctor workflows.

---

## 7. Connectivity, Digital Literacy & Accessibility

**Research insight:** Internet availability, digital literacy, infrastructure, usability and other implementation barriers can affect digital-health adoption.

**MediSync response:** Guided Assistant, multilingual access, voice interaction, Simple Mode and accessibility-oriented workflows.

---

## 8. Healthcare Equity

**Research insight:** Telehealth may improve access in non-urban settings, but equitable implementation requires attention to accessibility, availability, acceptability, affordability, adequacy and awareness.

**MediSync response:** A coordinated healthcare platform designed around rural and underserved users and their access barriers.
