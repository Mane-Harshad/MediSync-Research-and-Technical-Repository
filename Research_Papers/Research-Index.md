# MediSync — Research Index

This index documents the research resources reviewed during the design and development of **MediSync**.

The selected resources were examined to understand challenges and considerations related to rural and underserved healthcare access, primary healthcare, specialist availability, telemedicine, diagnostics, health records, healthcare-worker involvement, referrals, follow-up care, connectivity, digital accessibility, and healthcare equity.

# Research-to-Feature Overview

The research collection was used as a supporting source of evidence and context during the design of MediSync.

The relationship between the literature and the system is summarized below.

## 1. Rural & Underserved Healthcare Access

**Research insight:**  
Geographic and healthcare-service access barriers can affect people living in rural and underserved communities.

**MediSync design response:**  
Nearby Care, remote consultation and assisted healthcare workflows provide additional digital pathways for discovering and accessing available healthcare services.

---

## 2. Primary Healthcare & Specialist Access

**Research insight:**  
Digital-health and telemedicine interventions can help connect patients and primary-care settings with healthcare professionals beyond their immediate location.

**MediSync design response:**  
Doctor discovery, appointment scheduling and Video, Audio and Text consultation workflows provide multiple ways to connect with healthcare professionals.

---

## 3. Diagnostics & Service Coordination

**Research insight:**  
Primary-care facilities can face limitations related to diagnostic availability, service coordination and access to diagnostic expertise.

**MediSync design response:**  
The Diagnostics module allows users to select diagnostic services, book tests and coordinate appointments with available diagnostic facilities.

---

## 4. Health Records & Continuity of Care

**Research insight:**  
Timely access to organized and portable health information can support continuity of care when patients interact with different healthcare providers or facilities.

**MediSync design response:**  
Health Records, Clinical Records and Health QR provide structured access to selected patient health information.

---

## 5. Follow-up & Care Coordination

**Research insight:**  
Structured digital workflows can help organize continuing care and reduce dependence on fragmented or manual coordination.

**MediSync design response:**  
Appointment follow-ups and role-based care-management workflows support continued coordination between patients and healthcare professionals.

---

## 6. Health Workers & Assisted Care

**Research insight:**  
Community and frontline health workers can play an important role in supporting healthcare delivery and digital-health adoption in underserved settings.

**MediSync design response:**  
The dedicated Health Worker role provides workflows for assisting residents, supporting patient information and coordinating care-related activities.

---

## 7. Connectivity, Digital Literacy & Accessibility

**Research insight:**  
Internet availability, digital literacy, infrastructure and usability barriers can affect the adoption of digital-health systems.

**MediSync design response:**  
Guided Assistant, multilingual access, voice interaction, Simple Mode and other accessibility-oriented controls are incorporated to reduce usability barriers.

---

## 8. Healthcare Equity

**Research insight:**  
Telehealth can contribute to improved healthcare access, while equitable implementation requires attention to accessibility, availability, acceptability, affordability, adequacy and awareness.

**MediSync design response:**  
MediSync combines assisted access, multilingual interaction, remote consultation, healthcare-service discovery and coordinated care workflows with a focus on rural and underserved users.

# Research Use Disclaimer

The resources in this repository provide supporting literature and contextual evidence for the research and design of MediSync.

They are included to document the healthcare challenges, technology considerations and design areas examined during development.

They should **not** be interpreted as clinical, experimental or performance validation of the MediSync system.
